Try I'm In Golf

Imagine you are organising a friendly golf day. Try these two short tasks without help. There are no wrong answers — we want to find what feels confusing.

Use made-up names and an address ending @example.test. The email code is always 123456; no email will arrive. Everything resets if you refresh or close the page.

1. Organise your first golf day

Create your group, add yourself if you’re playing, preview an invitation and set up a free competition. Then practise entering scores, finishing the round and finding previous results.

Try being the organiserExample: Alex Taylor · Weekend Demo Golfers · alex@example.test. Practice controls can add fictional friends.

2. Join as an invited friend

Imagine a friend invited you to Saturday Demo Golfers. Verify a fictional email, enter your name and handicap, join the competition and submit your final score.

Try joining as a playerExample: Jamie Green · jamie@example.test · handicap 8.5. This is a separate fictional group, not the one from task 1.
