Designed for the fixture secretary

Plan the annual calendar, choose golf clubs, manage deposits, keep a record of communications and publish the member fixture list.

Competition scoring, handicaps, tee draws and Order of Merit are outside this ANGS fixture management feature.

Season planner

Captain's Weekend is established first. The fixture secretary chooses the number of events and can request suggested fortnightly Sunday dates. Suggested dates are guidance, not mandatory.

This showcase illustrates the planning workflow. No schedule is saved or published. Easter, Father's Day and other avoidable dates require the full scheduling rules before implementation.
