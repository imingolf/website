How it works

STEP 1

Join

Open your organiser’s invitation. Verify your email, then add your name and handicap if asked.

STEP 2

Get ready

Check the round and join before scoring starts. If there’s a fee, pay externally and confirm in the app.

STEP 3

Play

Enjoy your round without touching the app.

STEP 4

Score

Check and submit your final score. It then stays locked. See the results in your group.

A little help, whenever you need it

Ready to add your friends? How to invite players →

Start a group
  1. Create your group. Choose Start a golf group, enter your name, email, group name and handicap, then verify your email with the sign-in code. You’re added to the player list automatically.
  2. Invite your friends. Share your invitation and group PIN, or choose I’ll do this later.
  3. Set up payments. If collecting entry fees, add your payment link so players can pay you directly. You can choose I’ll do this later.
  4. Open your group. Tap Open my group to get started.

Use the same email when you return. You can invite friends and set up payments later from your group.

Invite your friends
  1. Open your group as the organiser.
  2. Open Group options → Invite players. You can also choose Invite players on the “Your group is ready” screen.
  3. Choose Share on WhatsApp, or Copy invitation message and paste it into your message to your friends.
  4. Each friend opens the invitation and signs in with their own email. New players add their own name and handicap.

Send the complete invitation privately. Joining the group and entering a particular round are separate steps.

Join as a player

Open the invitation your organiser sent you. Sign in using your email and the code sent to it. If you are new to the group, follow the prompts to add your name and handicap.

If you have a group PIN, choose Join my golf group in the app and follow the prompts. If joining is not offered, ask your organiser for the complete invitation.

For an existing player account, use the same verified email. You don’t choose somebody else’s name from a list.

Return to your group

Reopen the app. If asked to sign in, use the same email you used before.

Organiser: choose Start a golf group → Already registered? Sign in. Use the email you created your group with and verify it using the code emailed to you. Open your group, then use Group Admin to manage it.

Player: choose Join my golf group, use your group PIN if asked and sign in with your existing player email.

Joined in a browser and now using the iPhone app? Open the app from its icon and use the same PIN and verified email. Browser sign-in does not automatically transfer to the app.

Set up and enter a round

The organiser sets the course, date and competition in Round. Current game options include Money Game, Points Game and Trip Mode.

Players check the round details and follow the entry and payment prompts before scoring starts.

Once the first score is submitted, entries to that competition close. A friend can still join the group, but cannot enter that closed round. “Awaiting scores” means an existing entrant has not submitted their score yet.

Pay and confirm your payment
  1. Open Pay and use the organiser’s payment link.
  2. Complete the payment in your banking or payment service.
  3. Return to I’m In Golf and explicitly confirm that you have paid. Only confirm after actually paying.

I’m In Golf records declared payment status. It does not receive, hold or automatically verify the money.

Already entered, with a submitted score? You can still confirm your entry fee in Pay; your submitted score stays locked. If you have not paid, keep your status as unpaid.

Submit your final score

Open Scores after your round. Enter the final Stableford or Net total requested by the competition. Check it carefully and confirm submission.

Your submitted score is locked. Payment confirmation does not unlock it. If you made a genuine mistake, contact your organiser rather than submitting again.

The app uses final totals, not hole-by-hole scoring.

Find results and league standings

Use Scores for the current round’s scores. Once the organiser finishes the round, view the saved result and use League for standings.

Stableford points, Net totals and league points are different figures. Follow the label shown for your competition.

Trip basics

The organiser sets up the trip and its rounds in Trip Mode, including the competition format. Players follow each round’s entry, payment and score prompts.

View completed round results and the overall trip standings. Follow the selected format: Stableford uses points; Net uses net scores. The organiser starts each next round.

Need a little more help?

Check that you are using the same email as before and the latest sign-in code. Check your Inbox and Junk folder. If needed, use the app’s Send another code option when available.

For an invitation or group access issue, ask your organiser. For other help, email hello@imingolf.co.uk. Never send your sign-in code.
